User Instructions

  • Use this app to book garbage collection slots at your convenience.
  • Segregate your waste into Wet, Dry and Hazardous categories.
  • If waste is not segregated properly, fines may be imposed by the collector.
  • View past bookings and receipts from your dashboard.
Continue to Login

Collector Instructions

  • Login to view pending and confirmed bookings assigned to your area.
  • When you visit a user, confirm whether waste is segregated.
  • If segregated, mark the booking Completed; otherwise impose a fine.
  • After the user pays the fine, mark fine collection as confirmed in the app.
