Instructions

Please read the instructions carefully before using the application.

User Instructions

  • Use this app to book garbage collection slots at your convenience.
  • Segregate your waste properly into Wet Waste, Dry Waste, and Hazardous Waste.
  • If waste is not segregated properly, a fine will be imposed by the Garbage Collector and reflected in your payments.
  • Check your past bookings and receipts in the Dashboard.
  • Ensure your family members use the same login credentials for convenience.

Garbage Collector Instructions

  • Login to view all pending and confirmed bookings in your assigned location.
  • Upon visiting the user, confirm whether the waste is segregated or not segregated.
  • If segregated → mark the booking as Completed.
  • If not segregated → impose a fine through the app, which will be added to the user's account.
  • Confirm fine collection via the app once the user pays.

BBMP Official Instructions

  • Login to monitor overall garbage collection activities and compliance.
  • Set and update fee structures for garbage collection services.
  • Track fine collections and monitor non-segregation reports.

Admin Instructions

  • Manage users, garbage collectors, and BBMP officials in the system.
  • Oversee all bookings and payment records.
  • Ensure smooth functioning of the Book-A-Bin application.
